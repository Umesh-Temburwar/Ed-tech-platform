## 🚀 Day-of Interview Tips

### Morning (2 hours before)
1. Light review of INTERVIEW_QUICK_REFERENCE.md
2. Practice explaining main concepts
3. Mentally walk through architecture
4. Do something relaxing to calm nerves

### 30 Minutes Before
1. Final quick reference review
2. Hydrate and use bathroom
3. Have pen/paper ready
4. Take 3 deep breaths

### During Interview
1. Listen carefully to questions
2. Take 2 seconds to think before answering
3. Speak clearly and confidently
4. Use examples from your project
5. Draw diagrams if helpful
6. Ask clarifying questions
7. Admit if you don't know something

- "Let me think about that..."
- "That's a good question..."
- "Based on my understanding..."
- "I would approach it like..."
